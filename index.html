Auspicious Calendar

Main Dates & Annual Festivals

✨

Important annual thithis, festivals, and divine celebrations celebrated with grand splendor.

Kumbham / Masi (February – March) Annual Grand Festival

Annual Vishnumaya Thira Mahotsavam

The monumental annual festival featuring traditional Thira, Roopakalam, special Kalam Ezhuthum Pattum, Guruthi pooja, and grand Annadhanam with thousands of visiting devotees.

Masi / Kumbham (Feb – Mar) All-Night Jagaran

Maha Shivaratri Special Pooja

Special rituals invoking the supreme paternal grace of Lord Shiva and Lord Vishnumaya with continuous abhishekams and chants.

Kanni / Purattasi (Sep – Oct) 9 Sacred Days

Sharad Navarathri Celebrations

Nine sacred days of Devi & Vishnumaya alankarams, culminating in auspicious Vijayadasami Vidyarambham initiation.

Vrischikam – Dhanu (Nov – Jan) 41 Days Mandala

Mandala Kalam & Makaravilakku Season

Intensive 41-day spiritual prayer period with special oil lamp illuminations, Guruthi pooja, and protective blessings for Ayyappa & Vishnumaya devotees.

Medam / Chithirai (April 14) New Year Auspicious Day

Vishu & Kani Darshanam

Auspicious Vishu Kani darshanam in the morning, Vishukaineettam blessings, and special sweet payasam naivedyam.

Monthly Thithi Every Fortnight

Pradosham & Monthly Sashti

Special abhishekams and archana performed on auspicious Pradosham and Sashti days for peace and obstacle clearance.

Travel Guide

Location & Ways to Reach the Temple

🧭

Convenient transit options to reach Pallampetty House, Mannanur, Shornur, Palakkad.

Travelling by Bus

From Palakkad, take a bus towards Guruvayur via Ottapalam. Get down at Koonathara Bus Stop. From there, take an Auto Rickshaw to reach Kalarikkal Sree Vishnumaya Temple.
🚌

Palakkad (via Ottapalam)

Board any bus from Palakkad traveling towards Guruvayur via Ottapalam.

🚏

Get down at Koonathara Bus Stop

Alight from the bus at Koonathara Bus Stop.

🛺

Take an Auto Rickshaw

From Koonathara Bus Stop, take an Auto Rickshaw to reach Kalarikkal Sree Vishnumaya Temple.

Reaching By Road & Highways

1

Highway Connectivity

Conveniently accessible via the Palakkad - Kulappully - Shornur road and connecting highways (NH 544 & NH 66). Well-paved roads lead straight to Mannanur and Kavalappara.

2

Bus Routes & Stop

Buses running between Palakkad and Guruvayur via Ottapalam stop at Koonathara Bus Stop. Devotees can take an auto rickshaw from Koonathara Bus Stop directly to the temple.

3

Driving Distances

From Shornur Town: ~6 km (approx. 10 mins) | From Ottapalam: ~16 km (approx. 25 mins) | From Palakkad: ~44 km (approx. 60 mins) | From Thrissur: ~48 km (approx. 65 mins).

4

Parking Facilities

Spacious parking available on the temple premises for cars and pilgrimage vehicles.

Reaching By Train (Rail Connectivity)

🚂

Nearest Major Junction: Shornur Junction (SRR)

Shornur Junction is one of the largest and most connected railway hubs in Kerala (just ~6 to 7 km away). All major superfast and express trains from Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Coimbatore, and Trivandrum halt here.

🚉

Local Station: Mannanur Railway Station

Mannanur passenger station is located just about 2 km from the temple, serviced by regional passenger trains.

🚖

Taxis & Auto-Rickshaws

Prepaid auto-rickshaws and taxis are available 24/7 at Shornur Railway Station (Exit Gate). Ask for 'Kalarikkal Vishnumaya Temple, Pallampetty, Mannanur'.

🚌

Local Bus Connection

Town buses leave from Shornur bus stand towards Kavalappara / Mannanur at frequent 15-minute intervals.

Devotee Guidelines

Temple Visiting Guidelines

📜

Kindly observe traditional customs when visiting for consultations and darshan.

Visiting Days for Remedies

Please plan your visit on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday, or Tuesday (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM) for problem-solving consultations and dosha nivaranam.

Traditional Attire

Devotees are requested to wear modest, traditional clean attire (Dhoti / Kurta for men, Saree / Salwar for women).

Prior Intimation

For special poojas or distant travel, devotees may contact the temple helpline in advance: +91 9946 728 098 / +91 8943 445 544.

Prasadam & Offerings

Sacred raksha, bhasmam, and prasadam are given after consultations for protection and obstacle removal.
